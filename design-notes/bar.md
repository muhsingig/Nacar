# bar.md — what the references actually do

References: apple.com homepage (landing), iPhone 18 Pro (phone), Apple Watch Ultra 4 (watch), AirPods Pro 3 (earbuds). Captured 23 Sep 2026 at 1440px wide.

Every line is checkable by looking at a rendered frame.

1. **The device is the hero.** In the first viewport the product photo takes up at least 40% of the frame. Text sits in one block *above* or *below* it (never on top of the device), made of a small product label, a display headline of at most 2 lines, and at most one line of subcopy.

2. **Type ratio is extreme and the scale is small.** The display headline is at least 4.5× body size (≥ 64px against 14–17px body). A single viewport contains no more than 3 type sizes, not counting the nav.

3. **One accent, rationed.** The filled accent color appears only on Buy / Learn more pills and never covers more than 2 elements per viewport. Every other surface is black, #111 or #1d1d1f, with text in #f5f5f7 or #86868b.

4. **28px containers, zero shadows.** Every contained image, tile or panel has a ~28px radius and no drop shadow. Sections are separated only by a change of surface tone (#000 ↔ #111/#1d1d1f) or by empty space.

5. **One idea per section, lots of air.** Each section is a heading (usually left-aligned at the content edge, 48–80px) followed by one media block or one row of tiles. At least 120px of empty vertical space sits between sections. No section holds two headlines of equal weight.

6. **Numbers are drawn, not written.** Specs appear as stat blocks: a hairline on top, a big number (48px+) and a grey caption of at most 2 short lines, in rows of 2 to 4. No bullet-point spec lists in the storytelling part of the page.

7. **Two-tier navigation and a buy path that's always visible.** There is a thin global bar (~44px, 12px labels) plus a sticky local bar with the product name on the left and a Buy pill on the right. The purchase cluster (price plus Buy) appears in the hero and again at the end of the page.
