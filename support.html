Quick answers.

FaroHow do I move everything from my old phone?

Turn on Faro and hold your old phone next to it. Faro shows a pattern on screen; scan it with the old phone and your photos, messages and apps come across over Wi‑Fi. Keep both phones charging while it runs.

FaroHow does Beacon SOS work without a signal?

With no mobile signal, Faro finds a satellite and sends where you are to emergency services and your emergency contacts. Stand in the open with a clear view of the sky, and follow the arrow on screen to keep the satellite in view.

PulsoHow long does the battery last?

Up to 72 hours with workouts, sleep and alerts, and up to 144 hours in low-power mode. A dual-band GPS workout runs for up to 36 hours. From empty, 45 minutes on the charger takes it to 80%.

PulsoCan I swim and dive with Pulso?

Yes. Pulso is rated to 100 m for dives and open water. Rinse it in fresh water after the sea or a pool, and dry the band before you charge.

OndaHow do I switch noise cancellation modes?

Touch and hold the pearl disc on either bud to move between Noise Cancellation, Adaptive and Transparency. Squeeze the stem to play or pause, and slide a finger along the grooves to change the volume.

BillingHow does no-cost EMI work?

On select cards you can split the price into 12 monthly payments with no interest: Faro from ₹9,992 a month, Pulso from ₹6,658 and Onda from ₹2,075. Your bank shows the plan at payment. Terms apply.
